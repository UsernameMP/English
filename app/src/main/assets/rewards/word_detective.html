Word Detective

8 Mini Mysteries for English learners

This reader was written for English Sprint. Read slowly, tap unknown words in the app before opening the book, and try to guess each answer before reading the solution.

1. The Missing Key

Mia put the classroom key on the teacher's desk. Ten minutes later it was gone. Leo had left the room, Anna was reading by the window, and Sam was cleaning the board. Then Mia noticed a tiny silver shape under Sam's eraser. The key had stuck to the magnetic eraser.

Mystery solved: nobody stole it.

2. The Silent Alarm

Ben's alarm clock rang every morning, but on Monday he heard nothing. The battery was new. The clock showed the correct time. His little sister smiled and pointed at his headphones. Ben had fallen asleep while listening to music.

Mystery solved: the alarm worked; Ben could not hear it.

3. The Wet Footprints

There were wet footprints in the hall, but it had not rained. Nora followed them to the kitchen and found the family dog beside an empty water bowl. The dog had stepped into spilled water and walked through the house.

Mystery solved: the footprints came from the dog's wet paws.

4. The Vanishing Sandwich

Tom left his sandwich on a bench and turned away to tie his shoe. When he looked back, the sandwich was gone. Nobody was nearby. Above him, a crow landed on a lamp post with a piece of bread in its beak.

Mystery solved: the crow was faster than Tom.

5. The Wrong Bus

Ella got on the usual bus after school, but the streets looked unfamiliar. She checked the number: 14. Her usual bus was 41. The two digits looked almost the same from far away.

Mystery solved: Ella read the route number too quickly.

6. The Cold Cup

Dad made tea and left it on the table. Five minutes later one cup was already cold, while another was still hot. The cold cup had a metal spoon inside it. The spoon carried heat away faster.

Mystery solved: the spoon helped the tea lose heat.

7. The Light in the Empty Room

A light was on in an empty classroom. The switch was off. Max looked outside and saw bright afternoon sun reflected from a window across the yard. The room only looked as if a lamp was on.

Mystery solved: reflected sunlight created the bright patch.

8. The Late Message

A message sent at 3:00 arrived at 3:20. The phone had good signal and the internet was working. Then Kate saw that the sender's phone showed 2:40. Its clock was twenty minutes slow.

Mystery solved: the message was not late; the sender's clock was wrong.

End of book. Good detectives look for evidence before choosing an answer.
