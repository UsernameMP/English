# VSOSh subject pilot expansion — v0.6.22

Five subject packs were selected from the interaction-mechanics study to maximize breadth while staying inside the high-value P0/P1 renderer set.

- Biology — experiment inference, choice, matching, process ordering.
- History — sources, contemporaries, chronology, matching.
- Geography — scale/data interpretation, numeric answers, matching, causal order. Spatial map mechanics remain a next-stage extension.
- Social Studies — formal inference, classification/matching, legal and political procedures.
- Ecology — true/false style decisions, ecological relations, causal chains, sustainability.

The 100 shipped tasks are original training analogues informed by school-stage VSOSh formats; they are not copied task statements. Source metadata therefore uses `VSOSh-inspired` and the existing owned-original license.
